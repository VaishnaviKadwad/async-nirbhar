# Campus Standard Operating Procedures

## CAMPUS-FIRE-3.1
**Title:** General Building Evacuation and Fire Alarm Response
**Procedure:**
1. Upon audible building alarm, cease all academic activities immediately.
2. Evacuate through primary external stairwells; do not use elevators under any circumstances.
3. Assemble at the primary sports ground perimeter away from building facades.
4. Floor wardens conduct roster headcounts and notify security of unaccounted occupants.

## CAMPUS-FIRE-3.2
**Title:** Suspected Electrical Fire / Smoke in Electrical Room
**Procedure:**
1. Upon report of smoke or burning odor near electrical rooms or breaker panels, treat as suspected electrical fire.
2. Restrict access to the electrical room corridor.
3. Dispatch a guard to verify the alarm status at the source.
4. Do not attempt to extinguish suspected electrical fires with water or aqueous foam.
5. Evacuate the immediate sector and alert the floor warden.
6. Cut main circuit breakers to the sector only if the electrical panel is safely accessible without entering smoke.
7. Call campus security dispatch (ext: 101) reporting exact block and room identifiers.
8. Secure the fire door to starve oxygen; do not re-enter until emergency clearance is issued.

## CAMPUS-FIRE-3.3
**Title:** Smoke Without Confirmed Flame Source
**Procedure:**
1. Dispatch nearest patrolling guard to locate origin and inspect HVAC registers.
2. Do not activate high-draw ventilation fans that could accelerate drafts.
3. If visibility drops below 10 meters or burning odor intensifies, trigger partial wing evacuation.
4. Stage dry-chemical powder (DCP) and CO2 extinguishers outside the sector entrance.

## CAMPUS-FIRE-3.4
**Title:** Commercial Kitchen & Mess Hall Grease Fire
**Procedure:**
1. Smother pan or fryer fires with metal lids or approved fire blankets; never use water.
2. Activate kitchen hood automatic suppression pull-station if flames reach ventilation ducts.
3. Shut off main LPG cylinder manifold supply valve located outside the kitchen perimeter.
4. Evacuate kitchen staff through rear utility exit.

## CAMPUS-LAB-2.1
**Title:** Chemical Acid Spill in Laboratory
**Procedure:**
1. Immediately escort exposed persons to emergency eye-wash or deluge shower; flush for 15 minutes.
2. Clear students from the affected laboratory bay and isolate ventilation.
3. Retrieve neutralizer kit and apply sodium bicarbonate outward to inward on the spill.
4. Consult Safety Data Sheet (SDS) and notify Chemical Safety Officer.

## CAMPUS-LAB-2.2
**Title:** Hazardous Gas Leak Detected
**Procedure:**
1. Press lab emergency shut-off button to cut pressurized gas manifold supply.
2. Open exterior windows only if safe; do not touch light switches or electrical toggles.
3. Evacuate laboratory and adjacent corridor immediately.
4. Post guard outside entrance preventing access until ambient meters read baseline levels.

## CAMPUS-MED-1.1
**Title:** Unresponsive Individual / Cardiac Arrest
**Procedure:**
1. Assess area safety; do not move casualty unless structural hazard exists.
2. Check carotid pulse and chest movement for no more than 10 seconds.
3. Call campus health center (ext: 108) and request urgent AED deployment.
4. Perform uninterrupted chest compressions at 100-120 bpm until medical team takes over.

## CAMPUS-MED-1.2
**Title:** Severe Heatstroke / Outdoor Dehydration
**Procedure:**
1. Move the casualty to an air-conditioned room or dense shade.
2. Loosen tight collar and footwear; elevate legs slightly.
3. Apply cool, wet towels to neck, armpits, and groin area.
4. Administer oral rehydration solution (ORS) only if individual is fully conscious.

## CAMPUS-RAG-1.1
**Title:** Reported Ragging Incident — First Response and Protection
**Procedure:**
1. Separate reporting student and implicated parties into distinct administrative offices.
2. Record initial confidential statement without forcing immediate face-to-face cross-examination.
3. Notify Anti-Ragging Committee chairperson within 2 hours of receipt.
4. Assign campus counselor and guarantee protected residential escort for the reporting student.

## CAMPUS-RAG-1.2
**Title:** Unauthorized Late-Night Hostel Gatherings
**Procedure:**
1. Hostel wardens must disperse non-resident groups gathered in freshman blocks after 22:00.
2. Log student identity cards and room numbers without physical confrontation.
3. Inspect common rooms and terrace doors; ensure electronic access control logs are archived.
4. Submit night inspection discrepancy report to Chief Warden office by 08:30 next morning.

## CAMPUS-RAG-1.3
**Title:** Cyber Harassment and Digital Intimidation
**Procedure:**
1. Advise victim to preserve full digital evidence (screenshots, URLs, sender handles).
2. Prohibit retaliatory messaging by either party.
3. Forward technical logs to Campus IT Security to trace local network IP or MAC addresses.
4. File formal inquiry report under the institutional student code of conduct.

## CAMPUS-SEC-4.1
**Title:** Unidentified Intruder / Perimeter Trespass
**Procedure:**
1. Security officers request physical or biometric university identification.
2. If individual refuses or exhibits aggressive posture, keep visual contact and maintain safe standoff.
3. Radio central gate control to halt visitor exit barrier operations.
4. Direct backup patrol unit to intercept and escort subject to security headquarters.

## CAMPUS-SEC-4.2
**Title:** Electronic Perimeter Fence / Sensor Alarm Trigger
**Procedure:**
1. Console operator pans PTZ security camera to active sensor zone coordinates.
2. Verify visual feed for wildlife or environmental false triggers within 45 seconds.
3. If feed shows human activity or cut fencing, dispatch quick reaction vehicle.
4. Log timestamp, camera clip ID, and physical inspection findings into security logbook.

## CAMPUS-SEC-4.3
**Title:** Physical Altercation or Crowd Disturbance
**Procedure:**
1. First arriving guard alerts control room before intervening.
2. Instruct onlookers to clear corridors and egress pathways.
3. Intervene with minimum necessary restraint to separate primary disputants.
4. Escort separated parties to proctor's office for documented mediation.

## CAMPUS-ENV-5.1
**Title:** Severe Storm and High-Wind Advisory
**Procedure:**
1. Secure ground-level loose objects, construction hoardings, and laboratory exterior waste bins.
2. Advise campus community to stay indoors and avoid standing under mature trees or light towers.
3. Switch ground-floor electrical distribution units to auxiliary sump pumps if water pools.
4. Maintain active VHF radio channel 3 for facilities and maintenance personnel.
