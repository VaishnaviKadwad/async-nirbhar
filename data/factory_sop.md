# Factory & Industrial Plant Standard Operating Procedures

## FACTORY-PPE-1.1
**Title:** Mandatory Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Compliance
**Procedure:**
1. Hard hats, high-visibility vests, and steel-toed boots required in all active fabrication zones.
2. Direct supervisors must halt assembly lines if an operator lacks certified eye protection.
3. Issue a safety non-compliance ticket and escort unequipped personnel to staging locker.

## FACTORY-HEAT-2.1
**Title:** Industrial Boiler Overheating / Pressure Spike
**Procedure:**
1. If boiler temperature exceeds 280°C or pressure exceeds 18 bar, trigger automated blowdown valve.
2. Sound localized steam evacuation horn across boiler bay 2.
3. Isolate main fuel gas manifold; switch to auxiliary circulating cooling pumps.
4. Barricade a 30-meter radius around the pressure vessel until ambient readings stabilize.

## FACTORY-CHEM-3.1
**Title:** Toxic Solvent / Acid Drum Puncture
**Procedure:**
1. Arriving responders must wear chemical vapor respirators (APF 50 minimum).
2. Deploy polypropylene absorbent berms around the drum perimeter to halt drain runoff.
3. Apply inert neutralizing compound (calcined clay or soda ash) over standing liquid.
4. Notify Environmental Health & Safety (EHS) dispatch (ext: 911).

## FACTORY-MACH-4.1
**Title:** Rotating Machinery Entanglement Hazard
**Procedure:**
1. Hit immediate emergency red E-Stop button located on machine console or safety cable trip line.
2. Do not attempt to reverse motor rotation manually if limbs or clothing are caught.
3. Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) breaker station 4B to prevent automatic restarts.
4. Administer basic first-aid pressure dressing and call on-site plant paramedic unit.

## FACTORY-FIRE-5.1
**Title:** Flammable Paint Booth / Aerosol Flash Fire
**Procedure:**
1. Trigger automatic dry-chemical deluge pull lever outside the spray booth.
2. Interruption switches must halt forced air intake and paint feed lines instantly.
3. Evacuate through East emergency roller shutter.
4. Use Class B foam extinguisher on external pools; do not open booth doors once sealed.

## FACTORY-GAS-6.1
**Title:** Compressed Gas Cylinder Valve Rupture
**Procedure:**
1. If cylinder begins hissing or rocketing, evacuate the immediate warehouse sector instantly.
2. Do not attempt to re-seat regulator threads under line pressure.
3. Clear high-bay crane transit paths and sound plant-wide siren.
4. Allow gas to discharge fully under fire suppression water mist spray if safe to monitor.

## FACTORY-ELEC-7.1
**Title:** Substation Arc Flash / High-Voltage Switchgear Fault
**Procedure:**
1. Maintain minimum 8-meter flash boundary from energized high-voltage cabinets.
2. Do not touch fallen overhead bus bars or metal railings connected to the switchyard.
3. Contact regional electrical utility grid control to de-energize incoming 11kV feed.
4. Dispatch industrial firefighting unit equipped with clean-agent extinguishers.

## FACTORY-ENV-8.1
**Title:** Exhaust Stack Particulate & Toxic Gas Exceedance
**Procedure:**
1. If continuous emission monitoring system (CEMS) flags opacity > 40%, throttle kiln feed.
2. Engage secondary baghouse filtration unit and electrostatic precipitators.
3. Log exceedance timestamp in plant environmental compliance register within 15 minutes.
4. If levels stay elevated past 30 minutes, initiate controlled kiln shutdown sequence.
